Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Fluenta Stories Development Team (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Fluenta Stories (the “App”).

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

When you create an account or sign in to the App, we may collect information such as:

  • your email address;
  • your name, if provided;
  • an internal user identifier;
  • authentication-related information needed to create and manage your account.

If you sign in using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we may receive account information from these services, such as your email address and name, depending on the information you choose to share and the settings provided by Google or Apple.

Apple Sign-In may allow you to share your real email address or use a private relay email address provided by Apple.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, passwords, or authentication tokens to our analytics provider.

App Usage Information

To provide the App’s features, save your learning progress, and understand how the App is used, we may collect and store information related to your activity in the App, including:

  • selected language and difficulty preferences;
  • saved or favorite audio exercises;
  • listening and learning progress;
  • interaction history;
  • screens viewed and interactions with App features;
  • subscription or access status, if paid features are introduced in the future.

Analytics Information

We use PostHog to collect limited product analytics that help us understand how users interact with the App and improve its features, performance, and usability.

Analytics information may include:

  • an automatically generated identifier for guest users;
  • our internal user identifier after sign-in;
  • screens viewed in the App;
  • interactions with audio exercises, subtitles, translations, favorites, categories, language settings, and sign-in prompts;
  • whether the App is being used by a guest or signed-in user;
  • device type and operating system;
  • App version and build information;
  • device locale, time zone, and general technical information;
  • analytics session information;
  • dates and times associated with analytics events.

Analytics is enabled by default.

You can disable or enable analytics at any time in the App by going to:

Profile → Legal & Privacy → Help Improve the App

Disabling analytics stops the App from sending new product analytics events from that installation. It does not affect the main learning features of the App.

Information collected before analytics was disabled may remain stored in accordance with our data retention practices.

The analytics preference is stored locally on your device. Reinstalling the App, clearing its local data, or using another device may reset this preference.

We do not intentionally send PostHog:

  • your name or email address;
  • passwords or authentication tokens;
  • audio, subtitle, or translation text;
  • full server responses;
  • signed content links;
  • payment card information.

PostHog Session Replay, Error Tracking, automatic touch tracking, automatic screen tracking, and GeoIP enrichment are disabled.

PostHog may still receive technical network information, such as an IP address, when your device communicates with its servers. We do not use PostHog to store precise location data or derived location properties such as your city or exact coordinates.

Technical Information

We may collect technical information about your device and use of the App, such as:

  • device type and operating system;
  • App version and build;
  • network and connection status;
  • general error categories;
  • security and authentication events;
  • server and operational logs;
  • general usage data needed to improve performance and fix issues.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as health information, political opinions, religious beliefs, biometric information, or precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • authenticate users and maintain signed-in sessions;
  • provide access to the App’s features;
  • save your preferences, favorites, and learning progress;
  • personalize your experience in the App;
  • provide audio exercises, translated subtitles, and other language-related features;
  • understand how the App and its features are used;
  • improve the App, fix bugs, and maintain security;
  • prevent abuse and technical misuse;
  • respond to your questions or support requests;
  • manage subscriptions or paid features, if they are introduced in the future;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your information for third-party behavioral advertising.

We do not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on users.

3. Payments and Subscriptions

At the moment, the App may be available without paid subscriptions.

If paid features or subscriptions are introduced in the future, payments will be processed through official app store platforms, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We do not directly collect or store your full payment card details. Payment processing, billing, refunds, and subscription management are handled by the relevant app store according to its own terms and privacy policy.

We may receive limited information from the applicable app store, such as subscription status, purchased plan, access entitlement, renewal, cancellation, or refund status.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, disclosure, or alteration.

These measures may include encrypted network connections, authentication safeguards, access restrictions, separation of development and production environments, and limits on the information sent to analytics services.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App’s services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, prevent abuse, and enforce our agreements.

Account information and account-linked App usage data are generally kept for as long as your account remains active.

Analytics information may be retained according to the settings and retention practices of our analytics provider.

If you disable analytics, the App will stop sending new product analytics events from that installation. Previously collected analytics information is not automatically deleted when analytics is disabled.

If you delete your account, we will remove the active account and associated data from our primary application systems within a reasonable period, unless certain information must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, payment, accounting, or operational reasons.

Some information may remain for a limited period in:

  • backups;
  • security or operational logs;
  • analytics records;
  • system records maintained by us or our service providers.

Deleting your account does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all previously collected analytics or technical records.

You may contact us to request deletion of additional personal information that can reasonably be linked to you.

Anonymous or aggregated information that can no longer reasonably be linked to you may be retained.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

You can delete your account inside the App by going to:

Profile → Delete Account

Deleting your account removes access to the account and begins deletion of associated account data from our primary application systems.

If you no longer have access to the App, or if you want to request deletion of additional personal information, you can contact us at:

fluentastories@gmail.com

Please contact us from the email address associated with your account and include “Delete my account” or “Delete my data” in the subject line.

After receiving your request, we may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and process the request.

Some deletion operations may take additional time because they are processed asynchronously or involve backups, logs, or third-party service providers.
