Delete Your Fluenta Stories Account

You can delete your Fluenta Stories account directly in the App by going to:

Profile → Delete Account

Deleting your account removes access to the account and begins deletion of associated account data from our primary application systems.

Request Account Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the App, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at:

fluentastories@gmail.com

Please contact us from the email address associated with your Fluenta Stories account and include “Delete my account” in the subject line.

After receiving your request, we may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before processing the deletion request.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the active account and associated account data from our primary application systems.

Some information may remain for a limited period in backups, security or operational logs, analytics records, or other system records where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

Anonymous or aggregated information that can no longer reasonably be linked to you may be retained.

For more information about how we handle and retain personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
